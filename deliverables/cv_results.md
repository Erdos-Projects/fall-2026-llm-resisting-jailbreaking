Each row is one hyperparameter setting. Each number is that metric averaged over the 5 folds, either on the training part of each fold or on the validation part. Rows are sorted by validation AUC. **Bold** rows are the settings in `CHOSEN_PARAMS` that `analysis.py` uses.

The rows are ordered by validation AUC, but the choice is determined by the validation AUC, F1, and how consistent the scores are between training and validation sets (e.g. avoiding those with 100% training accuracy).
